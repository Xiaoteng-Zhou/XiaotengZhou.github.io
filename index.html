About Me

I am an underwater robotics researcher working at the intersection of acoustic sensing, physical modelling and intelligent perception. I develop sonar-based methods for detecting, tracking, mapping and reconstructing underwater scenes when optical visibility is limited. My work combines analytical models and Python-based algorithms with water-tank experiments, towing-tank trials, field deployments and multisensor measurements.

My applications include underwater infrastructure inspection, marine-debris monitoring, plume tracking, benthic environmental investigation and ecological analysis. I completed my Ph.D. in Marine Acoustic Perception at The University of Tokyo and hold an M.E. in Ocean Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology.

Research

Sonar Perception & 3-D Reconstruction

Denoising, super-resolution, adaptive mosaicking and rapid 2.5-D/3-D reconstruction for underwater inspection and mapping.

Underwater Robotic Navigation

Sonar localisation and mapping, USBL fusion, acoustic-marker pose estimation and active viewpoint optimisation.

Acoustic Modelling & Simulation

Acoustic simulation with models of propagation, reflection, backscatter, multipath and clutter, supported by experimental validation.

Marine Environmental Sensing

Feature-based and AI-based detection, tracking and density mapping for debris, plumes, benthic environments and fluid phenomena.
